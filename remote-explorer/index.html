Remote Explorer: what the app actually sends and keeps

This section is written by the developer to list exactly what Remote Explorer does. It adds to the generated policy above, and where the two differ, this section is the precise description.

Sent to Sentry (crash reporting and performance monitoring)

  • Crash reports: the stack trace, device model, Android version, app version, a random per-install ID, and, when opening a file fails, the share name and file path.
  • Performance traces and load-time metrics.
  • UI interaction breadcrumbs (taps and scrolls).
  • A screenshot of the app at the time of a crash.

Not collected

No account, no ads, no advertising ID, no analytics SDK, no location and no contacts.

Stays on your device

SMB server addresses, usernames and passwords (encrypted with the Android Keystore), the files themselves, the thumbnail cache and the viewing history. None of these are sent to the developer.

Permissions

  • Network access, to reach your own SMB server.
  • All files access, used only to read the folder you pick as a "Folder on this device" connection.

Children

Remote Explorer is not directed at children. Its target audience is 18 and over.
