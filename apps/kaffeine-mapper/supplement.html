Kaffeine Mapper: what the site actually does with your data

This section is written by the developer to list exactly what Kaffeine Mapper does. It adds to the generated policy above, and where the two differ, this section is the precise description.

Your location history stays in your browser

The Google Timeline export you import is read and mapped in your browser. It is never uploaded to the developer, and it is not saved once you close the page.

Map tiles and icons

The map is drawn with tiles from OpenStreetMap, and its marker icons come from unpkg.com. Your browser requests these directly, so those services see your IP address, and OpenStreetMap sees which map tiles are loaded. Because the map shows the places you imported, those tiles reveal the general areas you visited, though not your individual visits or times. OpenStreetMap handles this under the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Not collected

No analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no account.
