Genshin Prefarm: what the site actually keeps

This section is written by the developer to list exactly what Genshin Prefarm does. It adds to the generated policy above, and where the two differ, this section is the precise description.

Cookies that remember your inputs

Two cookies remember your server and the numbers you entered, so they are still there next time. The site only reads them in your browser. Your browser also sends them with requests to GitHub Pages, which hosts the site and does not use them. Clearing this site's cookies removes them.

Not collected

No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no account, and no scripts or fonts loaded from other sites.
