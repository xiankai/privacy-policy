Google Play History Analyzer: what the app actually does with your data

This section is written by the developer to list exactly what Google Play History Analyzer does. It adds to the generated policy above, and where the two differ, this section is the precise description.

Everything runs in your browser

The app is a static web page hosted on GitHub Pages. Your Google Play purchase history file is read and analysed in your browser. It is never uploaded to the developer, and the developer has no server that receives it.

Google Sign-In and Google Drive (optional)

  • If you choose to load your file from Google Drive, the app asks Google for read-only access to your Drive (drive.readonly) and opens the Google Picker so you can choose your Google Takeout file.
  • The app downloads only the file you pick, directly from Google to your browser.
  • It also reads your Google account's email address, only to show which account is signed in.

Stored on your device

Your browser's local storage keeps the Google access token and its expiry time, your email address, a flag that you have signed in before, the ID of the Drive folder you last opened in the Picker, and your dark-mode setting. These stay in your browser, and clearing this site's data removes them. You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Not collected

No analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no account with the developer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Google Play History Analyzer's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to show you your own purchase history; it is not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not read by any person.
